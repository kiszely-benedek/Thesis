How to read the description below. Each node is one plant item (equipment, valve or instrument function), even if it is drawn on several sheets; sheets lists every sheet it is drawn on. Each edge is a direct connection between two items. send_to is material flow from source to target, also across sheets: lines that continue on another sheet are already joined, and such an edge has crossed_sheets (the sheet the line leaves, then the sheet it continues on). send_signal_to, control and measured_by are instrument wiring. Each edge reads source, relation, target: equipment measured_by the transmitter that senses it, a transmitter or controller send_signal_to the next instrument function, and an actuator control the valve it moves, so a control loop runs equipment -measured_by-> transmitter -send_signal_to-> controller -send_signal_to-> actuator -control-> valve. Actuator nodes have no tag. Items at the edge of this excerpt may have connections that are not shown.
