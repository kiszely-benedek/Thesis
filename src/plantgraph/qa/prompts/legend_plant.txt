How to read the description below. Each node is one plant item (equipment, valve or instrument function), even if it is drawn on several sheets; sheets lists every sheet it is drawn on. Each edge is a direct connection between two items. send_to is material flow from source to target, also across sheets: lines that continue on another sheet are already joined, and such an edge has crossed_sheets (the sheet the line leaves, then the sheet it continues on). send_signal_to, control and measured_by are instrument wiring. Items at the edge of this excerpt may have connections that are not shown.
