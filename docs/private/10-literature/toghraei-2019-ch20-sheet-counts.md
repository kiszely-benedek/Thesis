# Toghraei ch.20 example sheets: what one sheet holds

**What these sheets are.** They are textbook illustrations. Each shows one item or one system, drawn as a single P&ID sheet. They are not a sample of a plant's sheet set, so a match or a mismatch with v2.2 shows plausibility only. They are not a validation. This is the same caveat that §2 of the v2.2 design states.

**Connector kinds.**

| Code | Kind | What is on the other side |
|---|---|---|
| P | process | another equipment item or a process sheet (a tank, pump, exchanger or unit) |
| N | network | a relief, flare, drain or blowdown header or system, or a distribution or collection header named as a header |
| U | utility | a utility supply or return header that serves the item: steam, cooling or glycol media, utility air, seal water |
| S | signal | an instrument signal continued on another sheet |

## Beside generator v2.2

The v2.2 figures are measured by the T7b, T6 and T10 coder runs on seed 7 and seeds 1–5 (`3e42a6a`, `cc4d5ef`, `8b34921`). T12b measures equipment per process sheet for v2.2 by sheet kind.

| Measure | Toghraei ch.20 | v2.2 (100 sheets, seed 7) | v2.2 (1,000 sheets, seed 7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Network + interarea share of sheets | 20% (7/35) | 16.0% | 16.4% |
| Interarea sheets | 2 of 35 | 0 | 1 |
| Connectors per network sheet, mean / max | 8.4 / 10 | 14.1 / 19 | 14.5 / 19 |
| Tie-ins per header sheet, mean | ≈ 6–8 (sheets 13, 14, 27) | 11.6 | 11.85 |
| Off-page stubs per process sheet, mean / max | 3.88 / 12 | 7.67 / 12 | 8.16 / 18 |
| Equipment per process sheet, mean / max | 1.72 / 7 | T12b | T12b (v2.1: max 11) |

**Reading.**
- **The share of network sheets is close:** 16% in v2.2 against 20% in the textbook set.
- **v2.2 header sheets are fuller.** They carry about 12 tie-ins and 14 connectors, against about 7 tie-ins and 8 connectors. This follows from §17.14 N2 (one header per area and service). It stays inside the ≤ 21 stub band the real sheets set.
- **v2.2 process sheets have about twice the textbook's stubs.**
  - A textbook example shows one item in isolation, whereas a v2.2 process sheet carries a train fragment with its utilities.
  - The real-drawing samples (`real-pid-samples-2026-10.md`) measured 1–21 stubs per sheet, and v2.2 sits inside that range.
  - The textbook set is the low end, not the target.
- **The textbook has auxiliary sheets** (HVAC, FGS matrix, instrument details) **that v2.2 does not draw.** This is a stated limitation: the generator draws process, network and interarea sheets only.

**Reading.**

- **The connector mix on process sheets matches closely.** P is 61–62%, N 17–18% and U 11–12%, against 63 / 20 / 12%. S is about twice the textbook share: 9–10% against 5%.
- **The largest gap is process sheets with at least one N connector:** 98–99% against 60%.
  - In v2.2 nearly every process sheet ties a drain or a relief into a network.
  - A textbook example often omits its drains.
- **v2.2 process sheets hold about twice the equipment:** a mean of 3.1–3.3 items against 1.72, with a median of 3 against 1.
- **v2.2 network sheets are fuller:** a median of 16.5–19 connectors against 8.
