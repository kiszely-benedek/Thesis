## Claim
Computer vision detects pipe lines as **disconnected fragments**. This paper merges those fragments and builds equipment-to-line connections, producing a continuous connectivity graph.

## Evaluation
Short paper — 6 pages, 13 figures. Scope is within a single digitized drawing; the abstract says nothing about multi-page handling. No public dataset or large-scale numbers identified from the abstract.

## Relation to this dissertation
A technique note rather than a rival. Relevant because fragment merging is the same *class* of problem as off-page connector resolution, one level down: joining pieces that the extraction step left disconnected.

Worth reading properly **only if** we end up building our own detector rather than using Relationformer or working from synthetic DEXPI. Low priority for Phase 0–1.
