## Claim
Reframes P&ID digitization as **tag extraction + topology inference** rather than graphical reproduction. Two-stage MLLM workflow treating visual extraction and topology reconstruction as distinct reasoning stages, which they report yields "more accurate and structurally consistent process representations".

## Evaluation
**Two ANSI-standard P&ID case studies** of increasing complexity. That is the entire evaluation.

## Relation to this dissertation
**Found during the 2026-08-23 novelty sweep — it was not on the professor's list and neither of us knew about it.** Published July 2026, roughly one month before we started. It is the strongest evidence that this field moves monthly and that `/lit-gap-check` needs to run on a schedule.

Threat assessment: **adjacent, not overlapping.** It is a digitization front-end, single-sheet, evaluated on two drawings. It takes no territory from ADR-0001's scope. If anything it strengthens the case: another paper, another single-sheet evaluation.

Its two-stage decomposition (extract tags first, infer topology second) is worth considering for our own front-end — tag extraction is exactly what cross-sheet entity resolution needs, and getting tags out reliably is a prerequisite for linking equipment across drawings.
