## Claim
Predict symbols and their connections **jointly** with one transformer instead of running separate detectors and inferring topology afterwards. Ships PID2Graph, the first public P&ID benchmark with graph-level ground truth.

## Method
**Relationformer** on deformable DETR: ResNet-101 backbone, transformer encoder–decoder with N+1 tokens. An object-detection head predicts boxes and classes; a **relation head (MLP) scores every object-token pair** for an edge. Nodes and edges therefore come out of one forward pass.

Diagrams are ~4500×7000 px, so they are cut into patches with ≥50% overlap, then merged back in six steps (confidence adjustment, NMS, weighted box fusion, self-loop removal).

Baseline is the classic **modular pipeline**: Faster R-CNN symbols + CRAFT/EasyOCR text + Hough-transform/DBSCAN lines + graph assembly.

## Evaluation
Training: 700 synthetic templates → 170,944 patches; plus **only 60 manually annotated real P&IDs**.

Test sets: PID2Graph Synthetic, PID2Graph OPEN100 (12 real reactor plans, mean **382±125 nodes/plan**), Dataset-P&ID (500 synthetic, Paliwal et al.).

| Dataset | Method | Symbol mAP | Node AP | **Edge mAP** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OPEN100 (real) | Relationformer | 73.14 | 83.63 | **75.46** |
| OPEN100 (real) | Modular | 76.99 | 52.14 | **45.89** |
| Synthetic | Relationformer | 78.36 | 96.89 | **88.95** |
| Dataset-P&ID | Relationformer | 76.69 | 97.72 | **95.07** |

Joint prediction beats modular on real-world edges by >25 points.

## Relation to this dissertation
Source of both our **dataset** and our **error model**. The number to remember is **edge mAP 75.46% on real plans** — that is the realistic quality of a graph extracted from a scanned sheet.

Every GraphRAG result in this field (ChatP&ID included) assumes a *perfect* DEXPI graph. Nobody has asked what happens to retrieval when the graph is 75% correct. Injecting corruption at these measured rates and plotting the degradation is a cheap, defensible experiment (ADR-0004).

## Limitations they admit
- Patching truncates large symbols; fragments are hard to classify
- The catch-all "general" symbol class absorbs up to **43%** confusion
- OPEN100 has no dashed lines → generalization to varied line types untested
- Only 60 real annotated P&IDs
- Their edge metric "disproportionately penalizes incorrect edge predictions compared to missed edges"

## Reusable
PID2Graph itself; the patch/merge strategy; graph-level metrics (Node AP, Edge mAP) as digitization-quality measures; GraphML+NetworkX as the interchange format.
