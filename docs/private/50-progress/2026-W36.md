**Net effect on the manifest:** 22 pairs now (was 21) — one recovered (`1/73↔2/16`), none lost, two downgraded from a false or overstated confidence to an honest one. Still 96/96 accounted for.

**Pattern worth naming:** every error the user found was the *same* failure mode — trusting the nearest visible text to a flag instead of tracing the actual line the flag's own arrow connects to. Montage crops (narrow, automated, fixed-margin) are good for finding *that a connector exists* and reading its *own target box* (which sits inside the box, always reliable); they are not good enough on their own for reading a *nearby* line number when the local layout has more than one pipe running through the crop. The reliable method, demonstrated three times today, is: get the exact bbox from the graphml, crop *much* wider (6× width, 80px margin) directly from the full-resolution sheet PNG, and trace the arrow by eye in that wider context — not just read whatever text is closest in a pre-made narrow crop.

**Also clarified for the record (no code changes, just understanding):**
- The montage row tags (`1/24`, `2/17`, ...) are `sheet/raw_graphml_node_id` — the number is PID2Graph's own annotation-order ID across *all* labeled objects on the sheet (connectors, valves, crossings, arrows, ...; ~386 objects/sheet on average), not something we assigned and not sequential among connectors alone.
- `Side` (left/right) is pure page-position geometry; `Direction` (incoming/outgoing) is read from arrow orientation and is NOT implied by `Side` — confirmed today by `2/13` (left-side box, arrow points left/outgoing-style) vs `2/14` (left-side box, arrow points right/incoming-style) looking different despite being geometrically on the same side. This is why `direction` was left `None` on all 96 records rather than inferred.
- `SIZE-XXX-NNN-SPEC` labels are real internal line numbers, just project-local — they're not external standards or product codes, so they're not googleable, and that's expected, not a red flag.
- A duct/pipe drawn with a break-line (zigzag) and no PID/sheet reference (e.g. the "TURBINE EXHAUST DUCT" near `1/5`) is a same-page "too long to draw fully" convention, not a cross-sheet connector — correctly out of scope for this benchmark regardless of whether PID2Graph even labeled it.
